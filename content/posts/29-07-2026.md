## What is the point?

I've been picking game ideas that I think would be fun to play myself and fun to work on and ... well, that is a tough thing to balance! Often the game I would like to play (such as a new F-Zero or Starfox) are just not enjoyable for me to work on. 

The game ideas fell broadly into two categories of abandonment: 

* It wasn't fun to play and to make it fun would require a LOT of development time and building of systems and a repeatable gameplay loop (e.g. for the cozy ideas)
* It was fun to play, but would require quite a bit of assets and content to make it interesting looking and unique. 

Some ideas I worked on were also just finicky and I knew they would be buggy (such as the procedurally generated seagull game!) and for me personally I wanted to gravitate towards something simple and tight in its design.
